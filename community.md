# Community

The participation contract, in three sentences. **Contributing starts smaller than you think:** the cheapest real contribution is reporting an ambiguity you found while reading — a *finding* — and the library's first external issue produced a canonicalized methodology section, so the loop demonstrably works. **Decisions bind through review, not conversation:** findings route through the pressure-testing channel, fixes land in the pattern's Lineage notes, and nothing changes canon mid-build — the spec changes only through a review pass. **The license is Apache 2.0** — the patterns belong to everyone, which is the point of a commons.

The pages below are the contract in full: how to contribute, how admission and sealing will be governed as the library matures, and the architectural decisions deliberately still open.
