# Healthcare atoms

Freestanding primitives for clinical and healthcare administrative systems. Each atom carries the regulated-pattern conventions required by HIPAA, HL7 FHIR, and 21 CFR Part 11.

| Atom | Status | What it models |
|------|--------|----------------|
| [Clinical Observation](./clinical-observation.md) | unresolved | A single clinical measurement — vital sign, lab result, assessment score — with immutable attribution and an amendment/retraction trail |
